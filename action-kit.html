The At-Cost Cooperative Model

Download PDF Paper

The Core Challenge

Household balance sheets face systemic margin compression. Multi-tier retail distribution chains introduce speculative overhead and profit extraction at each stage, reducing the real purchasing power of working families.

The Cooperative Alternative

By shifting the distribution of core essentials to a member-owned, at-cost cooperative framework, fulfillment is capped to pure operating cost. Capital returns are replaced with direct consumer savings at the point of access.
