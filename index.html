The Mutualized At-Cost Economy

Restructuring essential public utilities, basic banking, universal healthcare, and risk pools into non-profit citizen cooperatives funded by a streamlined consumption model.

The Four Core Pillars

1

Mutualized Public Banking

Depository institutions operating at cost. Eliminates predatory interest spreads, excessive fees, and debt-service extraction on fundamental consumer credit and mortgages.

2

At-Cost Universal Healthcare

Decouples medical care from corporate profit margins and bureaucratic overhead. Delivers direct clinical services and essential prescription coverage at actual cost of delivery.

3

Unified Cooperative Risk Pools

Property, casualty, and life underwriting pooled at transparent statistical risk rates, removing the profit premium, speculative reserves, and aggressive claim denial apparatus.

4

Universal Consumption Revenue

A single, equitable consumption-based tax framework that simplifies the tax code, stabilizes public revenue, and shifts fiscal drag away from productive household labor.
