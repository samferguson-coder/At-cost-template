Structural Economic Reform

The Mutualized At-Cost Economy

Eliminating compounding intermediary extraction by placing core production, distribution, and financial infrastructure directly into member-owned, at-cost stewardship.

The Four Foundational Pillars

1

Member-Owned Finance & Credit

Publicly held credit unions, cooperative lending facilities, and mutualized insurance eliminating secondary securitization spreads and usurious debt overhead.

2

At-Cost Essential Supply Chains

Wholesale distribution direct from producers to consumer tables, stripping out multi-tiered intermediary markups across staple foodstuffs and retail goods.

3

Non-Extractive Housing & Land

Community land trusts and at-cost development financing that isolate residential shelter from institutional speculation and yield-chasing equity funds.

4

Universal Cooperative Healthcare

Provider-patient mutual networks delivering clinical care, pharmaceuticals, and diagnostics without private equity extraction or middleman friction.
