The 4 Pillars of an At-Cost Economy

Moving essential human overhead from extractive profit centers into mutualized, citizen-owned public utilities.

0.75% Public Credit

Replaces commercial mortgage and debt extraction with at-cost administrative issuance, stripping compounding private interest from primary residences.

Direct-Cost Healthcare

Mutualizes medical provisioning into a non-profit risk pool, removing administrative billing bloat, middleman denial incentives, and marketing overhead.

~42% baseline overhead cut

Non-Profit Risk Pools

Property and casualty coverage shifted to collective self-insurance reserves. Undrawn capital remains in the pool or refunds policyholders instead of funding dividends.

Zero shareholder extraction

Consumption Prebate

Eliminates personal income taxes and payroll deductions entirely, funding government via retail consumption paired with a monthly poverty-line rebate check.

100% untaxed basic necessities
