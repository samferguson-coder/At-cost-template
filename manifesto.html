At-Cost Economy

A macroeconomic architecture for mutualizing non-discretionary necessities, removing financialized rent extraction, and un-taxing human productivity.

01.

Executive Summary & Core Thesis

Modern market economies suffer from a systemic design flaw: the extraction of private rents from non-discretionary human necessities. When access to shelter, basic liquidity, healthcare, and risk mitigation is conditioned on maximizing shareholder yield, the result is structural price inflation, wage stagnation, and escalating household debt.

The At-Cost Economy is a macroeconomic architecture designed to eliminate extractive friction from the baseline foundations of society without compromising private enterprise, price signals, or personal liberty. By converting four non-discretionary sectors into citizen-owned, at-cost public trusts and replacing the administrative deadweight of the tax code with a consumption levy paired with a universal prebate, the model restores direct purchasing power to the household.

0.75% Public Credit

Eliminates compounding mortgage interest rents and financialized secondary debt extraction.

Direct Medical Trust

Strips administrative billing bloat, middleman denial incentives, and corporate overhead.

Mutualized Risk Reserves

Operates property and casualty pools at cost; undistributed capital stays in public reserve.

Consumption Prebate

Eliminates income/payroll tax entirely while untaxing basic necessities up to the poverty line.

02.

The Four Pillars of At-Cost Infrastructure

PILLAR I

Public Credit & Zero-Rent Amortization

The Problem: In a commercial fractional-reserve banking framework, primary residential mortgages extract 100% to 200% of the original principal in pure compound interest over a 30-year lifecycle. This capital does not produce physical wealth; it merely services financial rents.

The At-Cost Solution: Transition primary home lending and foundational public infrastructure financing to a public utility credit facility capped at 0.75% annualized administrative interest.

Mechanics: Loans are backed by real physical assets with zero speculative secondary securitization. Repayments return 100% of the principal directly to retirement of the credit issue, with the 0.75% fee covering loan officer underwriting, title registries, and software infrastructure.

PILLAR II

The Direct Medical Care Trust

The Problem: Third-party commercial payers create misaligned incentives: denial-of-care optimization, bloated billing departments (accounting for up to 30% of aggregate hospital operating costs), and monopolistic pharmaceutical pricing.

The At-Cost Solution: Medical provisioning is decoupled from for-profit underwriting and placed into an open Direct Health Trust.

Mechanics: Capital is distributed directly to provider networks and clinical operations based on open fee-for-service cost recovery plus capital depreciation. Eliminates prior authorizations, corporate executive equity payouts, and marketing overhead, delivering an immediate 42% baseline cost reduction.

PILLAR III

Mutualized Risk & Catastrophic Pools

The Problem: Property and casualty insurance firms operate as leveraged capital pools. In severe climate or economic events, claims are denied or premiums spike to protect corporate return on equity (ROE).

The At-Cost Solution: Universal casualty, auto, and property risk is pooled into a Mutual Citizen Reserve.

Mechanics: Premiums are calculated on rolling 10-year actuarial cost bands. Unclaimed funds remain inside the reserve or are distributed as annual premium credits. No external equity extraction occurs.

PILLAR IV

Automated Universal Consumption Tax & Prebate

The Problem: The progressive income tax system and payroll taxes (FICA) punish productivity, create vast compliance deadweight, and fail to capture wealth hoarded in offshore shelters or unrealized asset gains.

The At-Cost Solution: Complete repeal of personal income, corporate income, and payroll taxes in favor of a 30% inclusive retail consumption levy paired with a monthly Federal Poverty Line (FPL) Prebate.

Mechanics: Every legal resident receives an automated, unconditioned monthly cash payment equal to the 30% tax rate multiplied by the monthly poverty threshold. Spending up to the poverty line is 100% tax-free in net terms.
